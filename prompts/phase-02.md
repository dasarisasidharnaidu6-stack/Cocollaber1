## 1. Scope & Objective
Implement core REST endpoints, validation schemas, business logic modules, authentication & verification flows, and integration test suites.

## 2. Key Target Deliverables
- **Auth Module:** Registration, email verification flow, login/logout, password reset (`apps/api/app/modules/auth/`)
- **Member & Profile Module:** Profile CRUD, offers & seeks management (`apps/api/app/modules/members/`)
- **Opportunity Module:** Opportunity lifecycle CRUD, search & filtering endpoints (`apps/api/app/modules/opportunities/`)
- **Interactions:** Express interest, accept/decline, participation confirmation (`apps/api/app/modules/interactions/`)
- **Messaging:** 1-to-1 secure messaging thread endpoints (`apps/api/app/modules/messaging/`)
- **Moderation:** Reporting & admin action endpoints (`apps/api/app/modules/moderation/`)
- **API Documentation:** OpenAPI specification & `docs/02-api/`
